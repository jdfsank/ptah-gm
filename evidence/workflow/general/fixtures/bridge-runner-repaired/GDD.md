# Game Design Document — Bridge Runner

## Concept
A small Godot 4 GDScript side-view jump game. Move the cyan player at 300 px/s with the left/right arrow keys and jump with Space to cross three visible platforms and reach a green exit. Preserve the existing platforms and controls. Falling restarts the level, and R resets it during play or after winning. Restart must clear stale exit contact so the player remains in the playing state at spawn and can complete another attempt. Use a fixed 960x540 view, procedural shapes, one level, and no sound, external assets, plugins, or network access. Keep simulation, input actions, rendering, and interface responsibilities separate.

## Object Model
- `bridge-runner` (project): Bridge Runner. A self-contained Godot 4 GDScript 2D MVP with a single playable level. Sources: not bound yet.
- `main-scene` (scene): Main Scene. Contains the player, three platforms, exit, procedural renderer, and HUD. Coordinates level state, the shared restart path, and exit-contact validity after teleporting to spawn. Sources: scenes/main.tscn, scripts/level_controller.gd.
- `input-actions` (mechanic): Keyboard Input Actions. Define move_left for Left Arrow, move_right for Right Arrow, jump for Space, and reset_level for R. Translate input into movement, jump, and reset intent. Sources: scripts/input_actions.gd.
- `player` (entity): Player. A 24x32 rectangular CharacterBody2D rendered cyan (#00FFFF) so it is easier to track. Start centered at (100, 414) on the first platform. Read speed and jump-speed from the frozen fields through StudioRuntime.object_value(). Sources: scenes/main.tscn, scripts/player.gd.
- `player.speed` (number): Speed; current value `300`; editable `true`.
- `player.jump-speed` (number): Jump Speed; current value `420`; editable `true`.
- `horizontal-movement` (mechanic): Horizontal Movement. Apply left/right intent using the player's speed field. Stop horizontal movement when neither direction is held; opposing inputs cancel. Sources: scripts/player.gd.
- `jump` (mechanic): Grounded Jump. A fresh Space press while grounded sets upward velocity from jump-speed. Apply gravity of 1000 px/s² during physics updates. Holding Space does not cause repeated jumps. Sources: scripts/player.gd.
- `platform-one` (entity): Starting Platform. A visible solid rectangle at (40, 430), sized 260x24 pixels. Holds the initial player spawn. Sources: scenes/main.tscn.
- `platform-two` (entity): Middle Platform. A visible solid rectangle at (370, 350), sized 210x24 pixels. Provides the intermediate jump destination. Sources: scenes/main.tscn.
- `platform-three` (entity): Exit Platform. A visible solid rectangle at (650, 410), sized 270x24 pixels. Supports the green exit. Sources: scenes/main.tscn.
- `platform-collision` (rule): Solid Platform Collision. Use static platform collision shapes matching the rendered rectangles. Player physics handles landing and grounded detection. Gaps remain open, with no invisible floor. Sources: scenes/main.tscn, scripts/player.gd.
- `exit` (entity): Green Exit. A green rectangular Area2D at (860, 350), sized 40x60 pixels, resting on the third platform. Label it EXIT. Exit-contact handling must reject stale contact evidence after a restart teleport. Sources: scenes/main.tscn, scripts/level_controller.gd.
- `win` (mechanic): Reach Exit. When the player currently overlaps the exit during play, enter the won state, stop player motion, and display the win message and R restart instruction. Pre-reset overlap data, contact flags, or delayed callbacks must not trigger victory at spawn. Sources: scripts/level_controller.gd.
- `fall-restart` (mechanic): Fall Restart. During play, restart when the player's center passes y=600. Use the shared restart path to return to the initial spawn, clear velocity and stale exit contact, restore playing state, and show the normal HUD. Sources: scripts/level_controller.gd.
- `manual-reset` (mechanic): R Reset. R restores the initial level state from either playing or won state, including spawn, zero velocity, active controls, and the normal HUD. Clear stale exit contact so resetting after victory remains in playing state at spawn and permits another complete attempt. Sources: scripts/level_controller.gd.
- `level-state` (rule): Level State. Maintain playing and won states. Use one restart path for falling and R. Evaluate reset before other transitions and end transition processing for the reset tick. Invalidate exit-contact evidence from before the teleport and require valid current contact for any later win. Suppress falling and movement after winning until reset. Sources: scripts/level_controller.gd.
- `fixed-view` (rule): Fixed View. Use a fixed 960x540 logical viewport showing the entire level. Do not follow the player or scroll. Preserve aspect ratio when the window changes size. Sources: scenes/main.tscn, scripts/view_setup.gd.
- `procedural-rendering` (mechanic): Procedural Shapes. Render the background, cyan (#00FFFF) player, existing platforms, and green exit using simple Godot shapes and colors. Keep drawing separate from physics, input handling, and HUD text. Sources: scenes/main.tscn, scripts/procedural_visuals.gd.
- `hud` (interface): Controls and Win HUD. Show: Left/Right: move \| Space: jump \| R: reset. Explain the goal: Cross the platforms to the green exit. On victory, show: You win! Press R to restart. Keep text clear of the jump route. Sources: scenes/main.tscn, scripts/hud.gd.

## Requirements
- `supported-scope`: Implement one Godot 4 GDScript 2D level with procedural visuals, no sound, no external assets or plugins, and no network access. Do not perform hash checks during building. (objects: bridge-runner, main-scene, procedural-rendering; requested check: manual; verification state: pending).
- `scene-binding`: The main-scene object is bound to scenes/main.tscn. Keep every declared object and mechanism associated with its listed source bindings. (objects: main-scene; requested check: manual; verification state: pending).
- `player-fields`: Preserve player field IDs speed and jump-speed. Speed is editable numeric data with value 300, minimum 50, maximum 500. Jump-speed is editable numeric data with value 420, minimum 200, maximum 700. Game source reads these frozen values through StudioRuntime.object_value(). Do not modify backend-owned design/game.json or studio/runtime.gd. (objects: player, horizontal-movement, jump; requested check: manual; verification state: pending).
- `keyboard-controls`: Left and Right Arrow move the player horizontally; Space triggers a grounded jump; R resets the level. Opposing direction inputs cancel, and holding Space does not produce repeated jumps. (objects: input-actions, horizontal-movement, jump, manual-reset; requested check: manual; verification state: pending).
- `three-platform-route`: Show exactly three solid platforms simultaneously. Preserve their existing positions and sizes. With speed 300 px/s and jump-speed 420 px/s, the player should be able to jump from the starting platform to the middle platform and then to the exit platform. Manually assess reachability and report any concern without changing the platforms. (objects: player, jump, platform-one, platform-two, platform-three, platform-collision; requested check: manual; verification state: pending).
- `solid-landings`: The player lands reliably on matching platform collision shapes and can fall through the open gaps. Grounded detection permits jumping only while supported. (objects: player, jump, platform-collision; requested check: manual; verification state: pending).
- `green-exit-win`: Place a clearly visible green exit on the third platform. Current player overlap during play changes the level to won, stops player movement, and displays a win message with an R restart instruction. Preserve normal victory on subsequent attempts. (objects: exit, win, level-state, hud; requested check: manual; verification state: pending).
- `fall-recovery`: Falling below the level during play automatically restores the initial spawn, zero velocity, playing state, and normal HUD through the shared restart path. Clear stale exit contact during this restart. (objects: player, fall-restart, level-state, hud; requested check: manual; verification state: pending).
- `reset-recovery`: R restores the same initial state during play and after winning. The player can complete another attempt after either reset path. (objects: manual-reset, level-state, player, hud; requested check: manual; verification state: pending).
- `fixed-camera`: Use a fixed 960x540 logical view with all three platforms and the exit visible together. The view does not follow the player or scroll. (objects: fixed-view, main-scene; requested check: manual; verification state: pending).
- `hud-guidance`: The HUD explains arrow movement, Space jumping, R reset, and the green-exit goal. Win feedback is readable and does not obscure the platform route. (objects: hud, win; requested check: manual; verification state: pending).
- `separate-responsibilities`: Keep physics and level simulation separate from input action mapping, procedural rendering, and interface updates. Input supplies intent; rendering reflects state; HUD presents controls and outcomes. (objects: player, level-state, input-actions, procedural-rendering, hud; requested check: manual; verification state: pending).
- `cyan-player`: Render the player cyan (#00FFFF) to make it easier to track. Preserve its 24x32 dimensions, collision shape, and initial spawn. Preserve all other existing visual elements. (objects: player, procedural-rendering; requested check: manual; verification state: pending).
- `preserve-existing-behavior`: Limit the requested implementation change to fixing restart and exit-contact handling for the reported reset-after-win regression. Preserve player speed 300 px/s, cyan rendering, existing platforms, controls, jump speed 420 px/s, gravity 1000 px/s², collisions, normal win behavior, both reset paths, fixed view, and HUD. Use the existing object and source bindings. (objects: player, input-actions, horizontal-movement, jump, platform-one, platform-two, platform-three, platform-collision, exit, win, fall-restart, manual-reset, level-state, fixed-view, procedural-rendering, hud; requested check: manual; verification state: pending).
- `reset-exit-contact-safety`: Give reset priority over other state transitions and end transition processing for the reset tick. Restart must invalidate remembered exit contact and reject overlap data or delayed contact callbacks belonging to the pre-teleport state. Returning to spawn must not trigger WON without current player contact with the exit. Contact evaluation must recover safely after physics synchronization so reaching the exit on a later attempt still wins. (objects: main-scene, exit, win, manual-reset, fall-restart, level-state; requested check: manual; verification state: pending).
- `manual-reset-after-win`: Manually reach the exit using actual Left/Right and Space input, observe the normal win, and press R while won. Confirm the player returns centered at (100, 414) with zero velocity at reset, playing state, active controls, and the normal HUD. Observe subsequent physics frames without movement input: the state must remain playing at spawn despite any stale exit contact. Then complete another attempt and confirm normal victory. Repeat win/reset cycles and report any recurrence of WON at spawn, including the reported state=1 with exitOverlap=false symptom. (objects: main-scene, input-actions, player, exit, win, manual-reset, level-state, hud; requested check: manual; verification state: pending).
