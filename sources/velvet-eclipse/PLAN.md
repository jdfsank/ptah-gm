**Tag:** v0.1.0

## Game Description

A silver-violet-haired sword hunter explores a cursed casino castle. The route begins at the Grand Lobby, crosses the Card Banquet Gallery, and climbs the Roulette Clocktower to obtain Moonstep. Double jump makes a previously unreachable Lobby balcony accessible on the return journey. Beyond it lie the Quiet Save Room and House Throne, where the Royal Dealer's defeat breaks the curse.

Movement, deliberate sword timing, limited card casting and invulnerable dodge support readable fights against three enemy kinds. Illustrated architecture, warm candles, wine curtains, gold trim and purple magic provide depth without disguising collision surfaces. All gameplay state and behavior use the real gecs World, Entity, Component and System APIs. Nodes materialized beneath ECS entities provide Godot physics and rendering; menus and overlays use normal UI scenes.

## Tag Mechanics

- [v0.1.0-M1] A/D or Left/Right moves the heroine; Space or W jumps with coyote time and jump buffering. Solid floors and platforms support predictable landing, facing and room-bound camera movement.
- [v0.1.0-M2] J performs a timed directional sword attack, K throws a magic card with an energy cost/recovery limit, and Shift or L dodges with a bounded invulnerability window and cooldown. Hits, misses, hurt, recovery and enemy defeat have visible feedback.
- [v0.1.0-M3] Three distinct enemy kinds participate in real combat: a pursuing melee skeletal dealer, a ranged spectral dealer with a telegraphed cast, and a rigid flying cursed card with a readable approach/attack. Their damage, defeat and collision behavior persist through the ordinary combat path.
- [v0.1.0-M4] E interacts with readable prompts, connecting Grand Lobby, Card Banquet Gallery, Roulette Clocktower, Quiet Save Room and House Throne. Moonstep is acquired in the Clocktower and grants a real second airborne jump; returning through Gallery to Lobby is necessary to reach the formerly unreachable upper balcony.
- [v0.1.0-M5] The Quiet Save Room checkpoint heals and saves progression. Death stops play and offers retry from the checkpoint; Continue and browser reload restore a valid checkpoint, Moonstep and discovered rooms. Missing, unsupported or corrupt saves fail safely and never trap a run.
- [v0.1.0-M6] The Royal Dealer has two combat phases using telegraphed card fans and slam zones. The second phase changes attack cadence and arena lighting visibly; legitimate boss defeat produces the ending and an explicit restart path.
- [v0.1.0-M7] Title/Start/Continue, onboarding, health/card-energy/objective HUD, ability badge, interaction prompts, discovered-room map on M, pause on Escape, death/retry and ending make the entire run understandable. Pause freezes gameplay and gameplay input while menu controls remain active.

## Playable Unit

- **Player experience:** Start without Moonstep, learn the controls in the Lobby, fight through Gallery, climb Clocktower and obtain the relic. Use its second jump at the Clocktower exit, return through familiar rooms, and jump to the Lobby's high balcony. Enter the safe checkpoint room, save, confront both Royal Dealer phases, and end the curse. Death and reload are recoverable parts of this same journey.
- **Unit outcome:** Boss health reaches zero through ordinary attacks, transitions once to the ending, and offers restart. The failure outcome is player death followed by an explicit retry/return choice. A new run and valid Continue both reach playable state without a debug command.
- **Scenes involved:** Main bootstrap (`scenes/main.tscn`), Title Screen (`scenes/ui/title_screen.tscn`), GameWorld (`scenes/game_world.tscn`) with Grand Lobby (`data/rooms/grand_lobby.json`), Card Banquet Gallery (`data/rooms/card_banquet_gallery.json`), Roulette Clocktower (`data/rooms/roulette_clocktower.json`), Quiet Save Room (`data/rooms/quiet_save_room.json`), and House Throne (`data/rooms/house_throne.json`); HUD (`scenes/ui/hud.tscn`), Pause Overlay (`scenes/ui/pause_overlay.tscn`), Map Overlay (`scenes/ui/map_overlay.tscn`), Death Screen (`scenes/ui/death_screen.tscn`) and Ending Screen (`scenes/ui/ending_screen.tscn`). The five named room scenes are data-driven ECS room compositions within GameWorld, not five bespoke gameplay-node controllers. SCENES.md must describe each composition separately.

## Risk Tasks

### R1. Establish ECS traversal, collision and resource materialization

- **Mechanics / player value:** [v0.1.0-M1], foundations of [v0.1.0-M2], [v0.1.0-M4] and [v0.1.0-M7]. The heroine moves, jumps, lands and visibly animates in the real Lobby with a stable camera and correctly aligned illustrated terrain.
- **Why isolated:** Engine-backed collision, asynchronous entity/node materialization, animation resources, platform reachability and camera limits can fail independently of high-level game rules. Resolve this before fighting/progression depend on it.
- **Prerequisite:** Finalized planning artifacts and the asset producer's verified runtime outputs. All required illustrated assets are generated before game workers bind them. No raster placeholder or procedural substitute is an acceptable asset-stage shortcut.
- **Approach:** Use real gecs World/Entity/Component/System queries and explicit input → intent → physics → feedback order. Materialize CharacterBody2D/StaticBody2D, collision shapes, AnimatedSprite2D/Sprite2D and the room camera from ECS components and room data. MovementSystem alone commits authoritative transform changes, including spawn/teleport requests and projectile/enemy movement added later. Input and AI write intent. Defer node creation/removal and room replacement outside physics callbacks. Separate sprite bounds, feet origin and collision bounds. Camera follows within room bounds without cinematic complexity.
- **Systems:** InputSystem, MovementSystem, MaterializationSystem, AnimationSystem and CameraSystem. They are one system per file under `scripts/systems/` using `{name}_system.gd`.
- **Component hints:** C_Transform, C_Motion, C_Body, C_Visual, C_Input, C_RoomMember, C_CameraTarget and C_Session. Data fields, types, defaults and ownership are defined in the subsequent STRUCTURE.md package; do not bypass that contract with node-local gameplay state.
- **Owned files:** `scripts/main.gd`, `scripts/runtime/game_runtime.gd`, `scripts/runtime/entity_factory.gd`, `scripts/runtime/room_catalog.gd`, `scripts/runtime/resource_catalog.gd`, `scripts/entities/game_entity.gd`; `scripts/systems/input_system.gd`, `movement_system.gd`, `materialization_system.gd`, `animation_system.gd`, `camera_system.gd`; `scripts/components/c_transform.gd`, `c_motion.gd`, `c_body.gd`, `c_visual.gd`, `c_input.gd`, `c_room_member.gd`, `c_camera_target.gd`, `c_session.gd`; `data/rooms/grand_lobby.json`; `scenes/main.tscn`, `scenes/game_world.tscn`; `project.godot` InputMap only; matching `test/test_<system_or_runtime_name>.gd` files.
- **Integration point:** Runnable Main → GameWorld with a real ECS player and Lobby, using the existing `run/main_scene`. Expose `simulate_action(action, pressed)`, read-only `get_run_snapshot()` and `get_player_snapshot()` through GameRuntime. - **Verify:**

  - Engine key events move and jump the on-screen heroine through the real InputMap; native output shows real frame changes and aligned feet/platform tops.
  - Measure and record achievable single/double-jump envelopes. Reserve a Lobby balcony gap above single-jump reach, below double-jump reach, and unreachable by all allowed pre-relic movement including dodge.

  - Missing/mismatched asset resources block binding rather than selecting a fake fallback. Relevant gdUnit tests and native import pass; no `e2e/` edits by the worker.
