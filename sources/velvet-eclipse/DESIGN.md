# DESIGN: Velvet Eclipse / 绯月赌城

## Visual Identity

A moonlit gothic casino castle in rich illustrated, cel-shaded fantasy art. Indigo stone, burgundy curtains and aged-gold ornament surround a silver-violet-haired hunter; warm candles and luminous purple cards guide the eye toward the playable foreground.

Sources: GDD.md §§4–7 and the eight inspected, user-supplied concepts identified in ASSETS.md. Their palette, character identity and architectural motifs guide original art; their pixel treatment, incidental lettering and extra systems do not expand the approved game.

## Image Style

### 1. Medium And Rendering

Use expressive non-pixel illustration with clear character poses and ornate painted architecture.
- Preserve illustrated shading and costume folds rather than reducing actors to geometric symbols.
- Keep heroine, dealers, props and scenery within one coherent rendering family.
- Do not claim exact pixel-art or one-to-one reproduction of the concepts.

### 2. Color And Value

Dark indigo and wine-red surroundings make gold, candlelight and magic accents legible.
- Use burgundy curtains, purple moonlight and fine aged-gold trim throughout the castle.
- Reserve bright magenta/pink for readable card magic and selected emphasis.
- Distinguish the spectral dealer with the references' cool cyan/green spectral treatment.

### 3. Shape And Silhouette

Gothic arches and layered balconies frame identifiable, separated combat silhouettes.
- Keep the heroine's short silver-violet hair, dark hunter outfit, flowing wine/violet accents and slender gold-accented sword recognizable.
- Give skeletal dealers tall, readable silhouettes with role-specific equipment and spectral treatment.
- Let the Royal Dealer dominate through a large regal skeletal silhouette without hiding the heroine.

### 4. Line And Edge

Crisp actor contours and fine decorative edges distinguish the foreground from distant architecture.
- Keep sword, hands, card corners and character outlines readable against dark stone.
- Concentrate intricate linework in gold borders, carved fixtures and costume accents, as in the supplied concepts.
- Do not simulate pixel art by quantizing or pixelating the illustration.

### 5. Material And Texture

Stone, velvet, metal and spectral glow should read as different materials.
- Give masonry carved depth, curtains visible folds and gold trim fine worn highlights.
- Use warm wax-light accents around chandeliers, candles and the save shrine.
- Keep magical cards and the ghost hand luminous rather than stone-like.

### 6. Lighting And Shadow

Cool moonlight and warm candlelight coexist, with combat effects remaining distinct.
- Use purple/blue ambient light behind warm local candle highlights.
- Light platform tops clearly enough to identify landing surfaces.
- Make boss phase two visibly change arena lighting without obscuring attacks or health information.

### 7. Space, Perspective, And Depth

The game reads as side-on action layered over deep castle interiors.
- Keep distant arches, windows and towers behind independent actors and foreground platforms.
- Separate foreground rails, curtains and chandeliers into distinct depth layers.
- No source concept may replace the assembled interactive room as a full-screen gameplay image.

### 8. Composition And Visual Hierarchy

Heroine, threats, landing edges and the next usable interaction lead the composition.
- Preserve clear space around the current combat and jump corridor.
- Distinguish Lobby, Gallery and Save Room through foreground arrangement and lighting even when their distant plate is shared.
- Keep prompts and HUD clear of important platform edges and attack warnings.

### 9. Detail Density And Abstraction

Rich architectural detail supports a concise, readable playable layer.
- Preserve casino identity through card suits, curtains, roulette imagery and gold ornament.
- Simplify tiny decorative detail before sacrificing character pose or projectile clarity.
- Treat chips as visual dressing; their presence must not imply an inventory or currency system.

## UI Visual Language

Gold/purple frames and card-suit motifs carry the castle's visual identity into clear Chinese-first UI.
- Use dark surfaces and restrained gold trim around important information.
- Give health and card energy visually distinct bars with readable text, rather than color alone.
- Keep Chinese labels prominent; the game title may be bilingual.
- Use readable headings, short labels and separated controls; avoid the concepts' dense miniature text.
- Differentiate selected, disabled and active controls visibly.
- Make the discovered map, Moonstep badge, interaction prompts and save status truthful to current state.
- Do not add portrait, inventory, shop or costume-selection panels from the reference montage.

## Do

- Reuse the references' identifiable mood and motifs through original illustrated assets.
- Preserve a genuine visual change when Moonstep is acquired and when the boss enters phase two.
- Make damage, recovery, defeat and a safe checkpoint understandable through visuals.

## Don't

- Substitute flat shapes, still actor cutouts or full-screen reference art for required illustrated assets and animation.
- Bake gameplay actors, HUD, interactive relics or collision surfaces into distant backdrops.
- Copy incidental reference branding, promise extra costumes, or imply gambling/payment functionality.
